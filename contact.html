Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about garment codes, shipping, and Mystery Box rewards.

Where do I locate the Mystery Box claim code on my t-shirt?
Every authentic Black Stone piece includes an embroidered collar satin tag. Turn the tag over or scratch the metallic security silver panel on the lower care label to reveal your 8-character unique alphanumeric redemption code (formatted as BLACKSTONE-XXXXX).
How does the 5-T-Shirt Milestone Grand Vault work?
Whenever you redeem 5 verified garment label codes in the Code Zone, you automatically trigger the Grand Vault Master Chest. This legendary drop awards heavyweight limited-run hoodies, sterling silver crest rings, and voucher credits.
What are your domestic and international shipping times?
All collection orders and Mystery Box physical rewards are shipped via express insured air courier. Domestic deliveries arrive within 2–4 business days. International dispatches take 5–8 business days with tracking provided upon dispatch.
What is your exchange and return policy?
We offer complimentary 14-day exchanges for sizing adjustments, provided garments are unworn with original woven tags and unscratched code labels intact. If a label code has already been claimed on the website, only store credit exchanges apply.
Do Mystery Box reward coupon codes expire?
Digital store credits and discount vouchers redeemed through the Mystery Box Lounge are valid for 180 days from the date of unboxing and apply across all upcoming drop collections.
